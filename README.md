An authentic Japanese culinary addon for **Kaleidoscope Cookery** on **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**!

Craft authentic nigiri sushi, slice whole fresh fish on interactive bamboo sushi mats, cook fluffy rolled tamagoyaki in stockpots, and serve your creations on traditional wooden sushi plates.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. 🍱 Interactive 3D Sushi Mat (`Cooking Mat` / `Sushi Mat`)
Experience true in-world sushi making! No standard GUI grids required—place ingredients directly onto the bamboo mat and watch your dishes take form in 3D:

- **Whole Fish Slicing directly on Mat**:
  - Place a whole raw fish (**Raw Salmon**, **Raw Cod**, or **Pufferfish**) onto an empty mat.
  - Slice with any kitchen knife into **4 sushi slices**!
  - *Note*: Slices must be gathered with an empty hand before starting sushi assembly.
- **Flexible Sushi Assembly**:
  - **Rice First Order**: Place cooked rice or seasoned sushi rice $\rightarrow$ right-click with fish or tamago slice $\rightarrow$ sushi assembled!
  - **Fish First Order**: Place a fish or tamago slice on the mat $\rightarrow$ right-click with rice $\rightarrow$ sushi assembled!
- **Display & Plating**:
  - Place finished sushi directly onto an empty mat for decoration or food presentation.
  - Right-click with an empty hand at any time to pick up your completed sushi.
- **Shift + Right-Click Undo**:
  - Made a mistake? Sneak and right-click to safely disassemble and recover your ingredients!

### 2. 🍣 Sushi & Nigiri Selection

| Sushi | Main Ingredient | Preparation / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Salmon Sushi** | Salmon Sushi Slice + Sushi Rice | Classic rich and tender salmon nigiri. |
| **Cod Sushi** | Cod Sushi Slice + Sushi Rice | Delicate white fish nigiri. |
| **Fugu Nigiri** | Fugu Sushi Slice + Sushi Rice | Masterfully sliced non-toxic pufferfish delicacy! |
| **Tamago Sushi** | Tamago Slice + Sushi Rice + Dried Kelp | Sweet egg nigiri hand-bound with a crisp nori strip. |

---

### 3. 🍳 Hot Kitchen Dishes & Prep

- **Tamagoyaki (Japanese Rolled Omelette)**:
  - Cooked in the Stockpot with eggs and sugar.
  - Place on a Chopping Board to slice into **Tamagoyaki Slices** and **Tamago Sushi Slices**.
- **Sushi Rice Preparation**:
  - Shape cooked rice directly on the mat with an empty hand, or craft via recipes.
- **Tableware & Presentation**:
  - **Wooden Plate**: Placeable multi-tier serving plate for your sushi pieces.
  - **Sushi Disc**: Round ceramic serving platter for elegant dining setups.

## 📖 How to Make Sushi

### Method A: Rice First
1. Place a **Cooking Mat / Sushi Mat** on a tabletop.
2. Right-click the mat with **Cooked Rice** or **Sushi Rice**.
3. Right-click with your choice of **Salmon Slice**, **Cod Slice**, **Fugu Slice**, or **Tamago Slice**.
4. Right-click with an empty hand to pick up your finished Sushi!

### Method B: Fish First
1. Place your **Sushi Slice** on an empty mat.
2. Right-click with **Cooked Rice** or **Sushi Rice** to form the sushi.
3. Right-click with an empty hand to pick up!

*(For Tamago Sushi, place the slice on rice, right-click to combine, add Dried Kelp for nori strip, and right-click to wrap!)*

Drop `kaleidoscope_japanese_food-x.x.x.jar` into your Minecraft `mods` folder along with **Kaleidoscope Cookery**.
