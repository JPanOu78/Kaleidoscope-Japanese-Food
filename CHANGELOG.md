# Changelog

All notable changes to **Kaleidoscope Japanese Food** will be documented in this file.

---

## [0.0.1] - 2026-11-11

### 🎉 Initial Release for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)

#### Added
- **Interactive Cooking Mat / Sushi Mat**:
  - Full 3D in-world block rendering for all cooking steps (22 distinct block states).
  - Whole fish slicing mechanism: slice **Raw Salmon**, **Raw Cod**, or **Pufferfish** directly on the mat with any knife into 4 sushi slices.
  - Flexible crafting sequence:
    - **Rice First Order**: Cooked / Sushi Rice $\rightarrow$ Fish / Tamago slice $\rightarrow$ Sushi.
    - **Fish First Order**: Fish / Tamago slice $\rightarrow$ Cooked / Sushi Rice $\rightarrow$ Sushi.
  - Direct display & plating support: place finished sushi items directly onto an empty mat.
  - Shift + Right-Click undo system to safely retrieve ingredients.
- **Sushi & Delicacies**:
  - **Salmon Sushi** (ซูชิแซลมอน)
  - **Cod Sushi** (ซูชิปลาค็อด)
  - **Fugu Nigiri** (ซูชิปลาปักเป้าล้างพิษ)
  - **Tamago Sushi** (ซูชิไข่หวานพันสาหร่าย)
- **Prep & Cooked Dishes**:
  - **Tamagoyaki**: Traditional rolled omelette cooked in the Stockpot.
  - **Tamagoyaki Slice** & **Tamago Sushi Slice**: Chopping board slicing recipes.
  - **Sushi Rice**: Moldable seasoned rice for nigiri shaping.
- **Tableware & Presentation**:
  - **Wooden Plate**: Traditional wooden geta serving plate with progressive sushi piece placement.
  - **Sushi Disc**: Round ceramic-style sushi plate.
- **Localization**:
  - Full English (`en_us`) and Thai (`th_th`) language files.
- **Integration**:
  - Deep compatibility with **Kaleidoscope Cookery 1.6.0+** kitchen appliances.
