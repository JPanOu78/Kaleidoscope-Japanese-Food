## 📄 Main Project Description (HTML / Markdown)

### 🍱 Overview
**Kaleidoscope Japanese Food** is an authentic Japanese cuisine addon for **[Kaleidoscope Cookery](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/kaleidoscope-cookery)**, built for **Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge**.

Say goodbye to flat crafting table recipes—prepare your sushi directly in the world with fully interactive 3D bamboo cooking mats, kitchen knives, stockpots, and traditional tableware!

---

### 🌟 Key Features

#### 1. 🎋 Interactive 3D Bamboo Sushi Mat
- **True In-World Crafting**: No GUIs needed. Watch rice grains, sashimi slices, and nori strips appear dynamically as you work!
- **Whole Fish Slicing**: Place a whole **Raw Salmon**, **Raw Cod**, or **Pufferfish** on the mat, grab your knife, and slice it into 4 sashimi cuts.
- **Flexible Workflow**:
  - *Order A*: Place rice first $\rightarrow$ add fish slice $\rightarrow$ delicious sushi!
  - *Order B*: Place fish slice first $\rightarrow$ add rice $\rightarrow$ delicious sushi!
- **Plating & Display**: Place completed sushi directly onto the mat for dining room presentation.
- **Undo Anytime**: Sneak + Right-Click to safely undo any step and recover your ingredients.

#### 2. 🍣 Authentic Sushi Selection
- **Salmon Nigiri (ข้าวปั้นหน้าแซลมอน)**: Rich, tender raw salmon over seasoned sushi rice.
- **Cod Nigiri (ข้าวปั้นหน้าปลาค็อด)**: Delicate white-flesh cod nigiri.
- **Fugu Nigiri (ซูชิปลาปักเป้าล้างพิษ)**: Expertly prepared pufferfish delicacy.
- **Tamago Sushi (ซูชิไข่หวาน)**: Sweet layered omelette bound with crisp dried kelp nori.

#### 3. 🍳 Hot Kitchen Delicacies & Tableware
- **Tamagoyaki (ไข่ม้วนญี่ปุ่น)**: Cook fluffy rolled omelettes in the Stockpot and slice them on the Chopping Board.
- **Wooden Plate (ถาดไม้)**: Traditional Japanese sushi serving geta that holds and displays sushi pieces.
- **Sushi Disc (จานซูชิ)**: Ceramic dining plate.
