# 🍣 Kaleidoscope Japanese Food

An authentic Japanese culinary addon for **Kaleidoscope Cookery**, built for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**!

Say goodbye to flat crafting table menus—prepare authentic nigiri sushi directly in the world with interactive 3D bamboo cooking mats, kitchen knives, stockpots, and traditional tableware!

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎋 1. Interactive 3D Bamboo Sushi Mat
- **True In-World Crafting**: Watch rice grains, sashimi cuts, and nori strips appear dynamically in 3D right on the mat as you assemble them!
- **Whole Fish Slicing**: Place a whole **Raw Salmon**, **Raw Cod**, or **Pufferfish** on the mat, grab any kitchen knife, and slice it into 4 sashimi portions.
  *(Note: Gather the 4 slices with an empty hand before beginning sushi assembly)*
- **Flexible Workflow**:
  - **Rice First Order**: Place cooked or sushi rice $\rightarrow$ right-click with fish/tamago slice $\rightarrow$ delicious sushi!
  - **Fish First Order**: Place fish/tamago slice $\rightarrow$ right-click with rice $\rightarrow$ delicious sushi!
- **Plating & Display**: Place completed sushi pieces directly onto the mat for dining room presentation.
- **Undo Anytime**: Sneak + Right-Click to safely undo any step and recover your ingredients.

---

### 🍣 2. Authentic Nigiri Sushi Selection
- **Salmon Nigiri**: Rich, tender raw salmon over seasoned sushi rice.
- **Cod Nigiri**: Delicate white-flesh cod nigiri.
- **Fugu Nigiri**: Delicately prepared non-toxic pufferfish delicacy.
- **Tamago Sushi**: Sweet rolled omelette nigiri bound with a crisp dried kelp nori strip.

---

### 🍳 3. Hot Kitchen Dishes & Tableware
- **Tamagoyaki**: Cook traditional sweet rolled omelettes in the Stockpot with eggs and sugar.
- **Chopping Board Slicing**: Slice rolled tamagoyaki into individual Tamagoyaki Slices or Tamago Sushi Slices.
- **Wooden Plate**: Traditional Japanese sushi geta plate that dynamically holds and displays multiple pieces of sushi.
- **Sushi Disc**: Ceramic dining platter for elegant restaurant setups.

---

## 📖 How to Make Sushi

### Method A: Rice First
1. Place a **Cooking Mat / Sushi Mat** on a tabletop.
2. Right-click the mat with **Cooked Rice** or **Sushi Rice**.
3. Right-click with your choice of **Salmon Slice**, **Cod Slice**, **Fugu Slice**, or **Tamago Slice**.
4. Right-click with an empty hand to pick up your finished Sushi!

### Method B: Fish First
1. Place your **Sushi Slice** on an empty mat.
2. Right-click with **Cooked Rice** or **Sushi Rice** to form the sushi.
3. Right-click with an empty hand to pick up!

*(For Tamago Sushi: place the tamago slice on rice, right-click to combine, add Dried Kelp for the nori strip, and right-click to wrap!)*
